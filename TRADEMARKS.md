# The agent-pack name

The code of agent-pack is free to use, modify and redistribute under its licenses. The **name** is not: "agent-pack" identifies the project created by Giuseppe Federico, and its use is governed by this policy.

## Allowed without asking

- Referring to agent-pack, accurately: articles, talks, tutorials, courses, comparisons.
- Saying that your work is **based on agent-pack** — required, in fact, by the licenses: *"Based on the Agent Pack paradigm by Giuseppe Federico (2026)."*
- Calling an implementation of the language **"compatible with agent-pack"**, when it follows the specification (`SPECIFICATION.md`) as published.
- Redistributing unmodified releases under their own name.

## Not allowed

- Naming a modified version, a fork or another product "agent-pack", or a name confusingly similar to it.
- Presenting a fork or a derived work as the official agent-pack, or as endorsed by Giuseppe Federico, when it is not.
- Removing or altering the attribution required by `NOTICE`.

A fork is welcome, under its own name.
