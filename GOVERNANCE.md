# Governance

agent-pack was created by **Giuseppe Federico**, who conceived the paradigm, designed the language and wrote the specification and the reference implementation.

## Founder and maintainer

Giuseppe Federico is the project's founder and its maintainer for life (*BDFL*). He has the final say on:

- the language and its specification (`SPECIFICATION.md`);
- the reference implementation and its releases;
- who is admitted as a contributor;
- the use of the name "agent-pack" (see `TRADEMARKS.md`).

## Contributing

Anyone may open an issue or propose an idea. Code contributions are accepted from contributors admitted by the maintainer, after they sign the Contributor License Agreement (`CLA.md`). A proposal becomes part of agent-pack only when the maintainer merges it.
